# Cookie Policy

_Last updated: May 22, 2026_

## 1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website.

## 2. How We Use Cookies

ApexPredix AI uses cookies for the following purposes: Authentication and session management, Customize your experience, Analytics.

## 3. Types of Cookies We Use

### Essential Cookies
Required for the Service to function (e.g., `apexpredix-session`).

### Performance Cookies
Aggregated analytics (e.g., `_ga`, `_gid`).

### Preference Cookies
Stores dark/light mode preference, language selection, regional pricing preference (e.g., `apexpredix-theme`, `apexpredix-language`, `apexpredix-region`).

### Analytics Cookies
Google Analytics (usage statistics).

## 4. Local Storage

We store the following in `localStorage`: theme, language, region, cookie-consent.

## 5. Managing Cookies

You can manage cookies via your browser settings or via our consent banner.

## 6. Third-Party Cookies

Analytics providers may set cookies. Each provider's policies apply.
