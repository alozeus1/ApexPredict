# Cookie Policy

_Last updated: May 22, 2026_

## 1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website.

## 2. How We Use Cookies

ApexPredict AI uses cookies for the following purposes: Authentication and session management, Customize your experience, Analytics.

## 3. Types of Cookies We Use

### Strictly Necessary Cookies
Required for the Service to function, including authentication, age gating, region gating, and consent storage.

### Analytics Cookies
Aggregated usage analytics, only after consent.

### Preferences Cookies
Theme, language, and regional pricing preference.

### Marketing-Future Cookies
Reserved for future campaign attribution. Not active at launch.

### Telegram-Notifications Cookies
Reserved for future Telegram alert preferences and notification state. Not active at launch.

## 4. Local Storage

We store the following in `localStorage`: theme, language, region, cookie-consent.

## 5. Managing Cookies

You can manage cookies via your browser settings or via our consent banner.

## 6. Third-Party Cookies

Analytics providers may set cookies. Each provider's policies apply.
